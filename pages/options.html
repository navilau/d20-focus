D20 Focus settings

Changes save as you make them. They apply from your next Start; a block that's already running keeps its rules.

Durations

roll × minutes

every breaks

A long break lasts as many minutes as you roll (1–20). Set to 0 to turn long breaks off.

Short breaks

Roll 2–4: 3 min · 5–8: 4 min · 9–12: 5 min · 13–16: 6 min · 17–19: 7 min.

Natural 20 on focus lets you extend by 10 min once. Natural 20 on a break gives 20 min; natural 1 gives 1 min. When off, 1 and 20 follow the normal rules. Re-rolling a 1 is always allowed.
